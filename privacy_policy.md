# Privacy Policy for NGFreshFoods

**Last updated: March 03, 2026**

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at **NGFreshFoods**. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us.

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our mobile application, **NGFreshFoods**, and any related services, sales, marketing, or events.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information You Disclose to Us
We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the App, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products/services, or otherwise contact us. The personal information we collect may include:
- **Contact Information:** Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number.
- **Account Details:** Profile picture (if any), Authentication data (via Firebase Phone/Email authentication).
- **Delivery Information:** Physical addresses, landmarks, and saved location details for delivering your grocery/food orders.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
Some information is collected automatically when you visit, use, or navigate the App. This information does not reveal your specific identity (like your name or contact information) but may include:
- **Device and Usage Information:** IP address, device characteristics, operating system, device models, and app usage logs/crash reports (via Expo or Firebase functionality).
- **Location Data:** We may request access/permission to track location-based information from your mobile device to facilitate accurate grocery delivery and feature local stock/availability.

### 1.3 Financial and Payment Information
All payment processing is handled securely by our third-party payment gateways. We do not store full credit card numbers or sensitive banking details on our servers, though we store order records, payment status, and selected payment method for your purchase history.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive for various business purposes, including to:
- **Facilitate Account Creation and Logon Process:** We use Firebase Auth to manage your sessions and verify your identity via phone/OTP or email.
- **Fulfill and Manage Your Orders:** To process carts, coupons, payments, and deliver products to your specified addresses.
- **Send Administrative Information to You:** Such as product updates, security alerts, and changes to our terms, conditions, and policies.
- **Protect Our Services:** To keep our App safe and secure (for example, fraud monitoring and prevention).
- **Respond to Inquiries/Support:** To resolve any issues you might have with your current or past orders.

## 3. Will Your Information Be Shared With Anyone?

We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, to protect your rights, or to fulfill business obligations.

Specifically, we may need to process or share your data with the following third parties:
- **Google Play Services:** For App distribution.
- **Firebase / Google Cloud:** For secure user authentication (phone and email data), database hosting, and app analytics.
- **Payment Providers:** To facilitate secure checkout and payment confirmation.
- **Delivery Partners:** To ensure your orders reach the correct location.

## 4. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We keep your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy notice unless otherwise required by law (such as tax, accounting, or other legal requirements). When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will either delete or anonymize such information.

## 5. Account Deletion and Data Rights

In compliance with Google Play Console policies, we provide a way for you to request the deletion of your account and personal data.
- **Data Deletion Link/Form:** Request deletion via our [Account Deletion Page](https://freshfarmfoods.co.in/delete-account) or email us at ngfreshfarm@gmail.com
- **In-App Deletion:** You have the right to delete your account directly from within the App's profile settings.
Upon request, we will delete or anonymize your personal information from our active databases, except for data required to maintain financial records or comply with legal obligations.

## 6. Do We Collect Information From Minors?

We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under 13 years of age. By using the App, you represent that you are at least 13 or that you are the parent or guardian of such a minor and consent to such minor dependent’s use of the App.

## 7. Security of Your Information

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures (including JWT tokens and secure APIs) designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## 8. Updates to This Notice

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Revised" date. We encourage you to review this privacy notice frequently to be informed of how we are protecting your information.
